Data Note - 2018 Uganda MIS (UGGE7IFL)

The GPS was collected and verified for all of the refugee clusters (clusters 321-342) except for two (324 and 329).
However, due to confidentiality issues, it was decided to not release the cluster locations. Therefore, all of the
GPS locations for the refugee clusters have been marked as WTH (Withheld) and have their location at (0, 0).
Researchers interested in tying these clusters to spatial covariates or other data may be interested in shdistrict
(Districts), the most precise location information found in the recode file.

If you have further questions, please contact us at gpsrequests@dhsprogram.com. Please note that all requests for
the locations of the withheld clusters will be denied.
